# Royal Egypt Group — Visual Identity Rules

## Master brand asset
- The official logo is the logo already used by the website: `/royal-logo.jpg`.
- Never redraw, reinterpret, regenerate, recolor, distort, crop, or replace the master logo.
- Use the original asset file for all designed/promotional graphics. Preserve its aspect ratio and clear space.
- If the logo file is unavailable to a workflow, stop and request/recover the original asset; do not invent a substitute.

## Required treatment for designed images
- Every designed image for Royal Egypt Group must carry the official logo in a consistent, clearly visible position.
- Default placement: lower-right corner for landscape/social graphics and upper-right corner for portrait graphics, with adequate safe margin.
- Keep logo size visually consistent (approximately 8–12% of canvas width, adjusted only for legibility); never let it dominate the subject.
- Use a subtle dark or light backing only when needed for contrast. Do not add effects to the logo itself.
- Keep a consistent premium palette: deep navy/black, restrained gold, and clean white.
- Keep typography and spacing consistent; do not generate embedded text when it can be typeset accurately in HTML/CSS or the publishing tool.
- Use one stable group identity across all divisions. Division names may appear as secondary labels, not as replacements for the master logo.

## Important distinction: promotional art vs. real listing/product photography
- Brand the designed promotional artwork, banners, campaign graphics, infographics, and social posts.
- Do not watermark or alter original customer/submitted property, product, transport, or documentary photographs. Preserve their authenticity; show the company identity in the surrounding card, caption, or frame instead.
- Never place a logo over important details, legal documents, property features, or evidence.

## Quality and integrity checks
Before publishing any designed image, verify:
1. The logo is the exact official asset from the website.
2. Aspect ratio, colors, and clear space are preserved.
3. The logo is legible at mobile size and not clipped.
4. No fabricated claims, prices, phone numbers, URLs, or decorative pseudo-text appear.
5. The image is appropriate to the correct group division and language.
6. The design remains legible in both mobile and desktop crops.

## Current website implementation
- The homepage hero uses the existing official logo asset.
- New campaign and generated images must follow these rules before they are added to the site or scheduled for publication.
